Decision (her, 30 Sep): only skills the student has count (A8 same skill or SBERT >= 0.8). Skill Gap is
strict and plain: have / to learn, with no "related" or "partly covered" reasons and no CV extras, because
closeness below 0.8 is not reliable evidence (9 of 60 above) and weak explanations mislead users
(Papenmeier et al. 2019). A partial credit (0.25-0.5) was rejected: any value is a judgement that can't be
measured without manual labels. Related knowledge still lifts a job in the Best-fit order through the
whole-profile SBERT similarity. Known cost: real matches below 0.8 are missed (e.g. Teamwork vs Team
collaboration, 0.76). Planned fix, to agree with the supervisor: fine-tune SBERT on skill pairs
(ESCO alternative labels, pairs mined from job ads, A8 pairs), evaluated on SkillMatch-1K.
