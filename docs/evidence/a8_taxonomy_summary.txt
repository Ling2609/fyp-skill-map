Conclusion: the ESCO-based taxonomy covers only ~15% of skill uses exactly, and SBERT-to-taxonomy mapping is not
precise enough. A8 therefore groups the database's own skill names (rules, then an LLM check of close pairs), and
keeps the taxonomy as a label where it matches exactly.
